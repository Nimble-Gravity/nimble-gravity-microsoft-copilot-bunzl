# M365 Copilot for Bunzl — Subject Brief

Read this before writing or revising lesson content. Captures who the program is for, the
agreed structure, and researched facts the content is grounded in. This brief replaces
`copilot-context.md` (the prior Brown & Brown engagement's brief) as the grounding document for
all Bunzl program content.

## 1. Engagement & positioning

- **Client:** Bunzl plc — a general/cross-functional population of M365 Copilot license
  holders spanning the business, not one department or function. Delivered by **Nimble
  Gravity** (site keeps NG branding; Bunzl appears as the client throughout).
- **Format:** three independent tracks, each **virtual/remote and recorded**, with
  capacity-capped registration handled by a separate external Nimble Gravity platform (the
  microsite links out to it; registration is not built into this site): **Foundations** (2
  hours, repeated on a recurring cadence), **Advanced** (2 hours, smaller champion/SME
  cohorts), **Governance** (1 hour, IT/security/compliance leaders). Structure per track:
  pre-work → live virtual session (recorded) → take-home depth, including that track's
  escape-room capstone. Copy should say "join the session" / "session recording," not "in the
  room" / "bring your laptop" — this program is not in-person.
- **Subject:** Foundations is rooted in core Microsoft 365 Copilot — what the paid Copilot add-on
  gives you beyond free Copilot Chat, Work IQ/grounding explained simply, everyday Chat and app
  basics. Advanced covers **Agent Build** (reusable tasks), **Copilot Cowork**, and **Copilot
  Studio**. Governance covers tenant controls, visibility/auditing, and agent governance for
  admins and compliance stakeholders.
- **Tone:** practitioner-to-practitioner, not marketing. Use Bunzl's own preferred collective
  term for its workforce: **"team members."** Public research found Bunzl's regional careers
  copy uses "team," "team members," and occasionally "teammates" interchangeably in
  culture/recruiting language, while its formal corporate and financial-disclosure materials
  (the "Bunzl at a glance" investor page, annual and ESG reports) label workforce headcount as
  "Employees." **"Team members"** is the confirmed default for this program's practitioner-facing
  copy (per the approved design spec); "employees" is acceptable only when directly quoting a
  formal disclosure figure. Frame AI as helping a fast-growing, decentralized,
  acquisition-heavy business absorb its workload — never as commentary on company performance.
- **Confidentiality:** every fact below is drawn from public sources — Bunzl's own investor
  site, press releases, and careers pages, plus Microsoft's own documentation and blogs — and
  is cited. No names, dollar figures, credit counts, or other specifics from any private
  discovery conversation appear anywhere in this repo; where a program design fact originated
  in a private discussion, it has been generalized here to an industry-standard pattern (e.g.
  "a consumption-based credit model," never a quoted figure).

## 3. Researched facts — Bunzl

### Company snapshot
- **Bunzl plc** (LSE: BNZL) — FTSE 100 constituent, headquartered in **London, UK**. Listed on
  the London Stock Exchange since 1957; FTSE 100 constituent since 1986. The Bunzl family
  business traces to a 19th-century Central European trading house; the modern UK distribution
  group was established in 1940 and has been purely a distribution/outsourcing business since a
  divestment programme completed in the 2000s. **CEO: Frank van Zanten**; **Chairman: Peter
  Ventress** (public board information, bunzl.com/about-us/our-leadership).
- **c.27,000 employees**, **c.150 operating companies**, present in **33 countries** across the
  Americas, Continental Europe, UK & Ireland, and Asia Pacific; **>15,000 global supplier
  relationships**; roughly 30% of group revenue is own-brand product (bunzl.com "Bunzl at a
  glance").
- **FY2025 (year ended 31 Dec 2025):** Group revenue **£11,845.4m** (+0.6% as reported, +3.0% at
  constant exchange rates; underlying revenue growth +0.4%). Adjusted operating profit **£910.3m**
  (down 4.3% at constant FX); adjusted operating margin **7.7%** (down from 8.3% in FY2024).
  Statutory operating profit £735.3m. (Bunzl plc, "Annual Results for 2025," bunzl.com/newsroom,

### Business model — value-added distribution, decentralized, buy-and-build
- Bunzl describes itself as **"the largest value-added distributor in the world in our market
  sectors"** — it sources, consolidates, and delivers customized product ranges from its
  supplier network so customers can simplify procurement and reduce the number of vendors they
  manage directly, rather than manufacturing the products itself.
- **Decentralized operating-company model:** the ~150 operating companies retain local
  commercial leadership and P&L accountability — Bunzl's own language describes the Group as
  "an organisation made up of local businesses, serving multiple sectors, across a wide range of
  geographies" — while the parent Group provides scale in sourcing, shared systems, and capital.
- **Growth strategy — organic plus "buy-and-build":** Bunzl has completed more than 200
  bolt-on acquisitions since the early 2000s, each typically folded into the decentralized
  model rather than centrally absorbed. FY2025 alone saw eight acquisitions announced with
  £132m of committed spend (bunzl.com "Annual Results for 2025"); the H1 2026 pre-close
  statement noted an active acquisition pipeline and completion of an Australian life-sciences
  distribution acquisition in April 2026. This steady drumbeat of newly onboarded operating
  companies — each bringing its own systems, reporting habits, and integration workload — is the
  natural, generalizable parallel to frame "why Copilot helps right now," in the same spirit
  the prior program used a single large acquisition's integration workload.

## 4. Researched facts — Microsoft 365 Copilot (verified 2026-08-16)

### The surface map (Foundations)
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat** is included free with M365 subscriptions: web-grounded chat,
  enterprise data protection, file upload, Pages, and agents on a pay-as-you-go basis. The paid
  **Microsoft 365 Copilot** add-on layers on work-grounded Chat (via Microsoft Graph), Copilot
  inside Word/Excel/PowerPoint/Outlook/Teams, Copilot Search, the **Researcher** and **Analyst**
  reasoning agents, scheduled prompts, memory, and Notebooks. Bunzl's licensed population should
  be taught the paid surface, with an explicit note on what free Chat lacks by comparison.
- **Grounding as signals, not a toggle:** teach it as controls, not a Work/Web switch — **Work
  IQ** (the Microsoft Graph context layer: mail, meetings, chats, files the user already has
  permission to see, plus any connectors), the prompt itself, files attached or referenced, the
  sources Copilot cites, and which agent is selected. Explicit web/public-source answers come
  from asking for them, not a mode switch. Checking citations before trusting an answer is the
  cross-cutting habit.
- **Model choice (2026):** a GPT-class default model, with **Anthropic Claude models
  selectable** for certain experiences where an admin has enabled Anthropic as a subprocessor
  (see the EU Data Boundary section below) — treat exact model names/versions as provisional;
  re-verify close to delivery rather than asserting a specific version in slide content.
- **Copilot Credits** are the metered currency ($0.01 per credit at pay-as-you-go rates) behind
  several premium/agentic experiences — Copilot Studio agents and Copilot Cowork chief among
  them — layered on top of the fixed per-user Copilot subscription. Admins allocate, budget, and
  cap credit spend centrally in the Microsoft 365 admin center's Cost Management dashboard.
  (learn.microsoft.com, "Usage-Based Billing and Cost Management for Copilot Credits," updated
  2026-08-13.)

### Copilot Studio (Advanced track)
- Low-code/no-code platform for building custom agents, sold as a **tenant-wide license**, with
  usage billed in pooled **Copilot Credits** rather than per user. As of the September 2025
  licensing shift, Copilot Studio moved from a per-message model to a **per-credit** model:
  different actions consume different numbers of credits (a scripted answer costs less than a
  generative-AI response; agent actions, tenant-graph grounding, and reasoning-model calls cost
  progressively more).
- Publicly quoted rates as of this research: pay-as-you-go around **$0.01 per credit** for
  standard usage (higher — publicly cited up to roughly $0.02–$0.04 — for premium/generative
  actions); a prepaid **capacity pack of 25,000 Copilot Credits costs $200/month** (~$0.008/credit
  effective); larger prepaid "Pre-Purchase Plan" commitments earn tiered discounts. A **Microsoft
  365 Copilot license is not required** to build/run standalone Copilot Studio agents — an Azure
  subscription is the only hard prerequisite. **Exact per-credit pricing and message/action
  weighting is one of the fastest-moving parts of Microsoft's Copilot licensing — treat every
  number above as provisional and re-verify against Microsoft's official Copilot Studio pricing
  page before quoting a figure in Advanced-track content.**

### Copilot Cowork (Advanced track)
- Microsoft's cloud-hosted agentic system: reached **general availability 2026-06-16** after a
  Frontier preview period. Where Copilot Chat answers questions and Copilot in the apps assists
  inside a document, **Cowork is handed an extended, multi-step, multi-app task and runs it to
  completion in a secure cloud-hosted environment** — work keeps running even if the user's
  device is off — returning a finished result rather than a draft or suggestion.
- **Requires a Microsoft 365 Copilot user subscription license as a prerequisite**; the
  subscription itself includes no Cowork entitlement — every Cowork task is billed separately
  and consumption-based, in Copilot Credits, at the same **$0.01/credit pay-as-you-go** rate (or
  a discounted prepaid commitment plan). Credit cost for a given task is driven by which model
  it uses, how much organizational context it retrieves, how many tools it calls, and how long
  it runs — Microsoft's own guidance buckets tasks informally into light/medium/heavy tiers by
  credit consumption rather than publishing a flat per-task price.
- **Off by default.** Admins explicitly enable Cowork, choose who gets access, set spending
  limits at the tenant, group, and user level, and configure usage alerts and reporting.
  (microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/blog, "Copilot Cowork is now generally available,"
  2026-06-16.) **This consumption model, and whatever allowance-group structure governs who at
  Bunzl can use Cowork, must be re-verified close to each Advanced-track delivery — do not
  quote a specific allowance figure in committed content.**

### Governance & tenant controls (Governance track)
- The **Copilot Control System** is Microsoft's admin framework for Copilot and agents, with
  three pillars: **security and governance**, **management controls**, and **measurement and
  reporting**. Controls are tiered "foundational" (Microsoft 365 admin center, SharePoint
  Advanced Management, Purview at an A3/E3/G3 license level) vs. "optimized" (Purview and
  Defender for Cloud Apps at an A5/E5/G5 license level) — a useful two-tier framing for the
  Governance-track audience.
- **Data security:** Purview Data Loss Prevention can, at the optimized tier, block Copilot and
  agents from processing or surfacing specific sensitive files outright; at the foundational
  tier, admins get oversharing notifications and can apply sensitivity labels manually.
  SharePoint Advanced Management and Purview data-access-governance reports help identify
  oversharing risk before it reaches Copilot.
- **AI security:** built-in protection against prompt-injection/jailbreak attacks and harmful
  content is on by default for every Copilot user, no extra license required. At the optimized
  tier, Purview Insider Risk Management can flag risky AI use (e.g., an attempted prompt
  injection or sensitive-data misuse) and adaptive protection can restrict a high-risk user's
  access automatically.
- **Compliance & privacy:** Purview eDiscovery can search and place legal holds on Copilot and
  agent prompts/responses (foundational tier); Purview Audit logs Copilot and agent
  interactions in detail; Communication Compliance and Compliance Manager add regulatory-
  framework tracking at the optimized tier.
- **Agent 365**, generally available in 2026, is the org-wide control plane for agents — it
  registers Copilot Studio agents as identities in Microsoft Entra so they can be governed with
  Conditional Access and role-based access control alongside human accounts, giving admins a
  single inventory of what agents exist, what they can access, and how they're behaving. This
  is a strong anchor for the Governance track's "visibility and auditing" content anchor.
  (learn.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/copilot/copilot-control-system/security-governance, updated
  2026-07-15.)
- **Baseline data-handling facts:** Copilot only surfaces content the signed-in user already has
  permission to see (identity-aware grounding); prompts, responses, and the Graph data Copilot
  reads are not used to train the underlying foundation models.

### EU Data Boundary & Anthropic models (re-verified 2026-08-16)
- Anthropic operates as a **Microsoft subprocessor** for Anthropic (Claude) models used across
  Microsoft 365 Copilot, Researcher, Copilot Studio, Power Platform, and Copilot in Microsoft
  365 apps.
- **Anthropic-model workloads are currently excluded from the EU Data Boundary** and, where
  applicable, from in-country data-processing commitments — this remains true as of this
  research date. Tenants inside the EU/EFTA and the UK have Anthropic models **disabled by
  default**; enabling them is an explicit admin opt-in (Microsoft 365 admin center → Copilot →
  "AI providers operating as Microsoft subprocessors").
- **Timeline (2026):** on 3 April, Microsoft introduced an EU/EFTA- and UK-specific admin
  setting to enable Anthropic models by default for Copilot in the M365 apps; starting 4 May,
  Anthropic became the default model for Copilot in Word, Excel, and PowerPoint in that region
  when the setting is turned on — with processing still occurring outside the EU Data Boundary.
  As of 22 July, a separate opt-in setting lets non-federal Government Community Cloud (GCC)
  customers use Anthropic models (still outside the FedRAMP-authorized boundary when enabled).
  Anthropic models remain unavailable entirely for GCC-federal, GCC High, DoD, and other
  sovereign-cloud customers. (learn.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/copilot/connect-to-ai-
  subprocessor, updated 2026-07-22 — primary source.)
- **Why this matters for Bunzl:** Continental Europe and UK & Ireland together are a meaningful
  share of group revenue (see §3 segment table), so Bunzl almost certainly has EU/EFTA and UK
  operating companies whose tenants (or tenant configuration) could be subject to this
  default-off behavior. **Whether Bunzl's EU/UK entities share a tenant with the rest of the
  business, and what the admin-level Anthropic setting is configured to at delivery time, needs
  tenant-level confirmation** (§5) — don't assert Anthropic availability for any specific Bunzl
  region without that confirmation.

### Carried-forward Foundations facts (from the prior program's research, re-dated)
- **Four-element prompting framework — Goal · Context · Expectations · Source** — is Microsoft's
  own official framework (support.microsoft.com, "Get better results with Copilot prompting"),
  plus: use positive instructions ("do," not "don't"), one goal per prompt, iterate, and
  reference specific files/people/meetings directly.
- **Scheduled prompts** (GA): any prompt run on a recurring schedule, output landing in chat
  history; works in Copilot Chat, Teams, and Outlook.
- **Copilot Search** (GA, no extra cost): semantic search across Graph plus 100+ connectors,
  answered by Copilot. **Copilot Memory** (GA rollout complete ~mid-2026): personalizes
  responses using saved memories and chat history, manageable on a dedicated settings page.
- **Teams intelligent recap** (GA): captures meeting notes, speaker attribution, and action
  items automatically — ask for the recap instead of asking a colleague to retype their notes.
- **Excel — quick orientation on an unfamiliar workbook**: Copilot can summarize a workbook,
  explain a formula in plain language, or highlight what changed since a prior version.
- **PowerPoint — outline-first drafting**: Copilot builds a stronger deck when given a
  structural outline first rather than being asked to generate a deck from a blank slide.
- These facts were true as of the prior program's 2026-07-31 research pass and were not
  contradicted by this pass's searches, but were not independently re-verified against a fresh
  primary source in this pass either — treat as **carried-forward, not freshly confirmed**, and
  re-check before quoting a GA date in committed copy.
