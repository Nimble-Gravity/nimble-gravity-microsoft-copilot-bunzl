What You'll Be Able to Do

From good prompts to working agents

Every attendee already has Copilot. This program is about using it like a pro: grounding it in your work data, driving it inside the apps where your work actually lives, and — for champions and SMEs — delegating real tasks to Copilot Cowork and building with Copilot Studio.

Who it's for

Bunzl team members across the business. Every attendee has an M365 Copilot license; Foundations is open to everyone, Advanced is for champions and SMEs, and Governance is for IT, security, and compliance leaders.

What you leave with

A prompt discipline you'll actually reuse, hands-on practice in the apps where your work lives, and — depending on your track — a reusable Cowork task, a Copilot Studio starting point, or a governance action plan, plus a take-home capstone to make it stick.
